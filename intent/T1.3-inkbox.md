## Why
Eyes reaches workers by text. The engine decides who gets an offer and who wins a claim; this package owns the wording, the Inkbox calls and the inbound webhook, so a worker's "YES" becomes `handleWorkerReply` only when Inkbox really sent it.
## What
- `createInkboxMessaging(env)`: SDK client from `INKBOX_API_KEY`, identity from `INKBOX_AGENT_HANDLE` (fetched lazily once, refetched after a failure); throws at construction if either is missing. All sends use `identity.sendText`; offer, capture and paid carry idempotency key `<kind>:<task_id>:<phone>`.
- Pure, exported templates `offerText`, `captureLinkText`, `alreadyClaimedText`, `paidText`. The offer is at most 159 chars; links, emails and 7+ digit phone-like runs are stripped from the agent's question and place, and both are clipped on grapheme boundaries.
- `parseReplyIntent`: whole-message keyword match after lowercasing and dropping punctuation, spaces and skin tones.
- `inkboxRoutes`: raw body, optional bearer, 300 s timestamp window, HMAC signature, then `text.received` and 1:1 `imessage.received` from an E.164 sender go to `handleWorkerReply`. Other events: 200, ignored. Service errors: logged with only the phone's last 4 digits and no body, answered 200.
- `scripts/send-test.ts <phone> [text]` for the first live send.
## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- SDK `verifyWebhook` (owns `whsec_` prefix handling) in a try/catch because it throws on a wrong-length signature; it skips the documented 300 s window, so that check is ours. Bearer compare is constant-time via SHA-256 digests.
- Await the service before answering, not fire-and-forget: Inkbox only logs the status, and awaiting keeps the work inside the request on any host, including the InsForge functions fallback (ADR-004).
- Whole-message intents, not first word: "no worries, on my way" must not decline, "yes but later" must not claim.
- Deadline shown in America/Los_Angeles: demo and workers are in San Francisco and the contract has no worker time zone.
- Group iMessages and tapbacks ignored: a reaction cannot say which offer it answers.
## Assumptions
- Signing per docs/vendor/inkbox.md and https://inkbox.ai/docs/api/phone/webhooks.md (headers, `{id}.{ts}.{raw_body}`, 300 s). Payload shapes from the SDK's `TextWebhookPayload` / `IMessageWebhookPayload` and the docs samples, not yet a live delivery.
## Verification
- `packages/comms/src/inkbox.test.ts`, 53 unit tests, no network: docs-shaped SMS and iMessage replies reach the service with the parsed intent; 401 with no service call for wrong key, tampered body, stale/future timestamp, truncated signature, each missing header, no signing key, missing/wrong bearer; 200 with no call for `text.delivered`, reactions, group iMessage, `call.ended`; a failing service still gets 200 and the log has no full phone or body; offers with long, emoji, URL, email and phone-laden input stay at or under 159 chars with no link, phone or lone surrogate; 28-row intent table.
- Mutations (no timestamp window, no scrub, no bearer, no signature, code-unit clipping, no group filter) each fail a test. `pnpm verify:fast`: pass. Live-verified: no (no Inkbox key yet).
## Known gaps
- No dedupe on event `id` (needs engine-owned storage); the engine's conditional claim makes a retried YES harmless, other retried replies are processed twice.
- Not live-checked: payload shape, whether START arrives as `text.received`, idempotency key limits. Run `scripts/send-test.ts` and log one real webhook once keys exist.
