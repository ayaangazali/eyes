## Why
`engine.adversarial.int.test.ts` failed 6 of 20 runs locally, across five tests. Line 971 got `paid` where it expected `verified`. `tokenOf` found no offer. `transfersFor` returned `[]`. All integration runs shared the `eyes` database. Logging showed a stuck task paid to the right account with no phone collision, while another worktree ran `coverage:all` on the same database. Its global `retryPayouts` paid this run's tasks. The fake clock always starts at 2026-10-17 19:00, so workers left by every earlier run keep fresh locations and take offer slots inside the 3 km dispatch radius. Phone collisions (about 0.1% per worker) and the `acct_fake_<id>` account (identical to what the fake creates) were not the cause.

## What changed
- `scripts/int-db.ts`: integration setup file. It creates `eyes_it_<random>` on the `DATABASE_URL` server, points `DATABASE_URL` at it for the file, and drops it with `force` after the file. Without `DATABASE_URL` it does nothing, so the suites still skip.
- `vitest.config.ts`: integration project loads it through `setupFiles`.

## Known gap
- A killed run leaves its `eyes_it_*` database behind. It is never reused, so tests are unaffected.

## Verification
- `engine.adversarial.int.test.ts`: 20 of 20 consecutive runs green, 40 of 40 tests each. Before: 14 of 20.
- `pnpm test:integration`: 3 of 3 consecutive runs green.
- No `eyes_it_*` databases left afterwards.
