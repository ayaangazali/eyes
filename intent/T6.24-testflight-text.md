## Why
A worker with no app texts the Eyes number and gets no way to install it. The TestFlight link does not exist until Apple approves the beta, so the link must be optional config.

## What
- `EYES_TESTFLIGHT_URL` (optional). When set it must match `https://testflight.apple.com/join/<code>`; when unset it is absent from config.
- Texts `INSTALL`, `DOWNLOAD`, or `TESTFLIGHT` (case-insensitive) reply with the install steps when set, and "The Eyes app isn't available yet. Text HERE to share your spot from the browser." when unset.
- `APP` adds "Don't have the app? <url>" as a second line when set.
- START's app-link text puts "Install the Eyes iPhone app first: <url>" before the app link when set.
- HELP mentions INSTALL.
- The worker `/app` page links "Install it with TestFlight" when `VITE_TESTFLIGHT_URL` is set at build time; otherwise the current text stays.

## Not in this change
- Setting `EYES_TESTFLIGHT_URL` in production. Unset until the beta link exists.
