## Why
After a worker sent a photo, the result card showed the title "Verified and paid" and then a body that began "Verified and paid. $3.00 is on its way to your account. Thank you!". The server message opens with the title (`packages/engine/src/index.ts`, the result copy), and the card printed both. The non-live build had the same repeat: title "Verified", body "Verified. $3.00 is on its way to you.".

## What
- `apps/web/worker/src/capture.ts`: `showResult` drops a leading "<title>. " from the server message before it fills `#message`. The title and the lead both come from the same OUTCOME entry, so they cannot drift apart.
- Paid card (live build): title "Verified and paid", body "$3.00 is on its way to your account. Thank you!".
- Other outcomes checked: "Sent, needs a quick review" has no repeated title in its message, so it is unchanged. Expired shows the closed-task copy with no title, so it is unchanged. "rejected" is not a task status.
- `apps/web/worker/e2e/capture.spec.ts`: the verified card asserts the body is exactly "$3.00 is on its way to you."; a new test shows a needs-review message whole.
- Payout pending (live build, status `verified`): the title is "Verified, payout pending", but none of the three server bodies starts with that title. The client strip did not apply, so each body began "Verified. " under a "Verified, …" title. `packages/engine/src/index.ts` drops the leading "Verified. " from all three bodies: retry ("Your $3.00 payout did not go through yet; it will be retried."), payout link already texted ("We texted you a link to set up payouts; …"), and no payout setup yet ("To get your $3.00, set up payouts here: …").

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- The paid and simulated cards are fixed on the client: `showResult` strips the repeated title, and `engine.int.test.ts` asserts the server wording. The payout-pending bodies are fixed in the engine instead, because their title is not a prefix of the body. No test asserted those three strings.
