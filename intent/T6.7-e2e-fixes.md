## Why
The live iMessage E2E (2026-10-17, tasks 1917245e… and ef593d4f…) showed `quote_task` at an ordinary place (37.761,-122.386, is_open) returning `allowed: false` with "The automatic safety review could not decide…", while `ask_eyes` with a real question at the same place was allowed, then verified and paid. Quote has no question text. The engine made one up (`"is open at <label>"`), and clef put that text in the unsure band. Without Claude configured, the unsure band is refused. An agent that obeys quote would never ask.

## What
- `packages/safety`: `createAssessTask` returns the rules verdict when `question` is empty. It does not call clef or Claude for an empty question. Calls with a non-empty question behave as before.
- `packages/engine`: `quoteTask` passes `question: ""`, so quote runs the deterministic rules on the place label plus the existing task-type, bounty, cap and balance checks. `askEyes` is unchanged and still runs the full classifier on the real question.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- Empty question as the "rules only" signal, rather than a new flag on `SafetyInput` or a `checkRules` dependency in the engine: this keeps the `AssessTask` contract and the engine's dependencies the same, and it is a one-line change in each package.
- Quote's description already says it "does not check your question: ask_eyes checks the question itself", so no schema text changes.

## Assumptions
- The place label alone does not need the AI classifier. Rules still block bad labels, and `ask_eyes` classifies label plus question together.
