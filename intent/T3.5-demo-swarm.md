## Why
Gate G3 is "the demo script (§13) passes 3 times in a row". `e2e/demo.spec.ts` turns that script into a machine check on fake providers, so a broken demo step fails CI instead of failing on stage. `scripts/swarm.ts` is the local half of the swarm demo mode: about a minute of simulated agents and workers around San Francisco so the dashboard map lights up and the ledger fills, without live keys.

## What
- `e2e/demo.spec.ts`, one rehearsal per §13 step on the real MCP server, engine, Postgres, Inkbox and Primitive webhook routes, worker capture page and dashboard server: MCP `quote_task` then `ask_eyes` for Blue Bottle, Mint Plaza (3 workers, $3.50 escrowed); the dashboard `/feed` has the task at the cafe's coordinates with `workers_notified: 3` and the rendered page's headline reads "3 workers notified near ..."; a signed `imessage.received` YES gets the capture link; the capture page (fake camera, GPS at the cafe) shows "Verified and paid"; `await_answer` returns `paid`, all checks true, distance under 150 m, cost 3.5; exactly one 300-cent fake transfer and exactly one escrow 350, release 300, fee 50, payout 300 ledger row for the task; the dashboard headline turns to "worker paid $3.00". Second test: "Photograph the house at 123 Oak St" must be `unsafe_task` with a `fix`. Third test: a signed Primitive `email.received` to the agent's Eyes address gets a threaded "Got it" reply, then (after a YES and a capture) a reply with `in_reply_to`/`references` set and the photo attached.
- `scripts/swarm.ts` (`pnpm swarm --agents 50 --tasks 2`): in-process engine on `DATABASE_URL` with fake providers; 40 workers (seeded PRNG, round-robin around 15 public SF places, opted in with fake payout accounts); N agents signed up ($20 test grant); N x tasks tasks at those places with matching task types and $2 to $5 bounties; fates 50% paid, 20% claimed, 15% cancelled, 15% expired, posted over 36 s and acted on 3 to 12 s later; prints each event and a summary read through `eyes_dashboard_feed`. Refuses `EYES_PROVIDERS=live` always, a missing `DATABASE_URL`, and a non-localhost `DATABASE_URL` unless `--allow-remote`.
- `package.json`: `test:demo` (`playwright test e2e/demo.spec.ts --repeat-each=3 --workers=1`) and `swarm`.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- Own database and ports per run: CI runs the e2e files on 2 workers, so `golden-path.spec.ts` and this spec start together. Golden assumes it is the only spec with workers at the cafe and binds 3399. The spec creates `eyes_demo_<hex>` on the `DATABASE_URL` server, migrates it, drops it in `afterAll`, and listens on ephemeral ports. The prebuilt capture page has `http://127.0.0.1:3399` baked in, so `page.route` forwards those requests to this run's API. Verified by running the whole suite with `--workers=2`: golden and demo ran side by side and both passed.
- `CREATE DATABASE` needs a raw client; files under `e2e/` resolve packages from the root only, which has no `postgres`. The spec loads `postgres` through `createRequire` anchored at `packages/db` instead of adding a root dependency (only the two script lines are allowed in `package.json`).
- Dashboard step: Leaflet and tiles come from third-party CDNs, so the spec aborts `unpkg.com` and asserts the pin through `/feed` (status, lat, lng, `workers_notified`) plus the page's headline, which renders without the map.
- Refusal: `@eyes/safety` on main is the `not_implemented` stub. Like golden, `beforeAll` probes it; while stubbed the service gets an allow verdict and the refusal test is `test.fixme(safetyStubbed, ...)`. It switches on by itself when T1.5 merges (checked with a refusing stand-in: 3 passed).
- iMessage, not SMS: the live demo uses the Inkbox iMessage line (#24), so the YES/START webhooks send `imessage.received`.
- Email test capture posts multipart straight to `/w/offers/:token/capture`; the page itself is covered by the first test.
- Swarm expiry: `deadline_minutes` is at least 5, so expiring tasks are posted with the clock set back so the deadline lands 3 to 12 s after posting, and `expireOverdue` runs then. Setting the clock forward instead would date the refund rows minutes into the future and keep them above a real demo's rows in the dashboard ledger.
- Swarm claims: `eyes_claim` gives a worker their newest open offer, so the swarm picks a notified worker whose newest open offer is this task, retrying every 2 s until 48 s. Without the retry, about half the claims found no such worker.
- Swarm safety is an allow-all verdict: questions come from fixed public-place templates and the real classifier needs a live key.

## Assumptions
- Inkbox signature as in golden: HMAC-SHA256 hex over `<request-id>.<timestamp>.<body>`; iMessage payload `data.message.{remote_number, content, is_group}` as parsed by `packages/comms/src/inkbox.ts`.
- Primitive signature `t=<unix>,v1=<hex HMAC of "<t>.<body>">` and the `email.received` shape from `packages/comms/src/primitive.test.ts`.
- The `DATABASE_URL` role may `CREATE DATABASE` (true for the CI service and `eyes-pg`).

## Known gaps
- Refusal step stays fixme until T1.5 (#7) merges.
- The spec reaches `postgres` through `packages/db`; adding `postgres@3.4.9` to the root devDependencies would be the plain import.
- With more than 50 swarm tasks, the dashboard's 50-task window leaves out the expired ones (posted with backdated clocks); their refund rows still show in the ledger.
- Swarm workers stay opted in near the demo places. Never point the swarm at the database a live demo dispatches from: they would take the nearest-3 slots. The guard blocks non-local databases unless `--allow-remote`.
- The Blaxel batch-job wrapper (T3.5, remote half) is not built.
