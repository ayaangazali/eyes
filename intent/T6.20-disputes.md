## Why
The operator: with the photo check moderation-only (T6.19), fake answers are handled by disputes. Confirmed fraud makes the agent whole and removes the worker; a rejected dispute leaves the worker paid.

## What
- Migration 021: `workers.banned`; ticket kind `dispute` with `dispute_status` (open/upheld/rejected) and `reversal_owed_cents`; one open ticket per (task, kind); ledger type `fraud_refund`; `eyes_confirm_fraud`, `eyes_record_reversal`, `eyes_reject_dispute`, `eyes_task_dispute`; `eyes_dispute` opens the ticket; banned phones can't opt in.
- `Payments.reverseTransfer` (Stripe transfer reversal, idempotency key `reverse-<task_id>`).
- `POST /ops/tickets/:id/confirm_fraud` and `/reject_dispute` `{ note }` behind the ops session + Origin check, logged as ops events (`warn` when a reversal is owed).
- `get_task.dispute { status, refunded_usd }`; `dispute_answer` description explains the outcomes.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
See ADR-026.

## Verification
- `packages/engine/src/engine.int.test.ts` (local Postgres, fake Stripe): dispute opens a ticket and get_task shows `open`; two concurrent confirm_fraud calls refund 350 cents once, reverse once, ban the worker, delete location codes, START gets the ban text, and a new task nearby finds no workers; a failed reversal records 300 cents owed and a second click reports it; reject closes once and the task stays paid; an unpaid needs_review fraud refunds through escrow with nothing to reverse.
- `apps/mcp/src/ops-auth.test.ts`: confirm_fraud needs a session (401), the same Origin (403) and a note (400), and logs a warn event.
- live-verified: see the PR #94 report.
