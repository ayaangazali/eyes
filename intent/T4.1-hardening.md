## Why
ADR-010 merged PR #11 (Primitive inbound) and PR #14 (apps/mcp request handling) with seven defense-in-depth findings deferred to feature freeze (#17-#23). This closes them, and adds the abuse control nobody filed yet: `POST /v1/signup` and `POST /w/join` are unauthenticated, and each signup gets a test grant that can text real workers.
## What
- #17 `parseTaskEmail`: `DIRECTIVE` is `/^\s*(location|at|type|bounty|deadline)\s*:(.*)$/i` (the value is trimmed after the match); Bounty strips `/^\$|(?:usd|dollars?)$/gi` and Deadline `/(?:minutes?|mins?)$/i`, and `Number()` drops the whitespace the old `\s*` runs scanned. A 20 KB space-padded line went from 0.3-1.4 s to about 1 ms.
- #18 sender-authentication table gains an unparsable From with an empty envelope sender: the sender address is `""`, `isTrustedSender` throws on the empty domain, and the catch must say untrusted.
- #19 the endless-stream test closes its stream after 20 chunks (20 MiB), so a broken size cap makes `send` resolve and the test fail instead of hanging.
- #20 `primitiveRoutes` processes at most 20 emails per agent per sliding hour, counted after dedupe; the rest answer 200 `rate_limited`, send nothing and log the agent id (never an address).
- #21/#22 the request log prints the matching route template (`/w/offers/<token>`, `/v1/tasks/<task_id>`, `/llms.txt`, ...) from `HTTP_ROUTES` plus the six served paths it lacks (`/`, `/agents.md`, `/robots.txt`, `/topup/done`, `/topup/cancel`, `/w/connect/done`), else `<other>`. No path byte is decoded or echoed.
- #23 `PORT`, when set, must match `/^\d+$/` and be 65535 or less.
- Throttle: `/v1/signup` and `/w/join` each allow 5 valid calls per sliding hour per client, keyed on route plus the right-most `X-Forwarded-For` entry; the node adapter fills `X-Forwarded-For` from the socket address only when the request has none. Over the limit: `EyesError("rate_limited", { retry_after_s })`, 429, with a fix naming the seconds and the UTC time to retry. At most 10,000 keys, oldest dropped first.
## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- #20 budgets inbound emails, not replies: each processed email sends at most two replies (ack + answer), so 20 emails bound replies at 40 per agent-hour, and a paid task's answer email is never the one dropped. Replying "over budget" was rejected: it is itself a reply a loop could trigger.
- #21 allowlist over better redaction: shape-based redaction missed `<B>..<M>`, `/<B>/<M>` and `%252E` (#22); a fixed template list cannot leak bytes it never copies. Templates come from the frozen `HTTP_ROUTES` so the list cannot drift from the contract.
- Throttle counts only requests that pass schema validation (a typo does not burn a slot) and checks and records synchronously after the body is read, so concurrent requests cannot all slip past the check. Signup and join have separate budgets: the golden e2e does 1 signup and 4 joins from 127.0.0.1.
- The socket fallback is done in the adapter by setting the header: the app then has one source (`X-Forwarded-For`) and the unit tests drive it without a socket.
## Assumptions
- The Manufact gateway appends the client address as the right-most `X-Forwarded-For` entry (task brief; docs/vendor/mcp-use-manufact.md does not document it). Deploy runs `main.ts` through `toNodeListener` (Dockerfile).
- `normalizeReceivedEmail` falls back to `mail_from` when the From header does not parse, and `isTrustedSender` throws a TypeError for an empty domain (SDK 2.4.0 `dist/trust-*.js`).

## Known gaps
- Both limits are in-memory and per process (one instance in the demo); a restart or a second instance resets or splits them.
- `X-Forwarded-For` is trusted as given: reached without the gateway, a client can pick its own key. IPv6 clients can rotate addresses within their prefix.
- The worker page shows its generic "HTTP 429" wording for `rate_limited` (apps/web/worker is not in this task's ownership).
- STATUS.md "Deferred" still lists #17-#23 (not in this task's ownership).
- Throttle key is the raw right-most XFF entry with no length cap: 10,000 keys of 8 KB cost about 80 MB; a blank XFF keys on "". Fix queued for the next apps/mcp task (truncate to 64 chars, /64 for IPv6, blank XFF as absent) (PR #37 minor).
- No test pins separate signup and join budgets; a shared-bucket mutant passes (PR #37 minor, queued with the above).
- Throttle runs after body parse on purpose (a typo does not burn a slot); an over-limit client still costs one parse of at most 1 MB.
