## Why
Operator feedback: the live START text showed "1." in front of a single step once payouts were set up, and offered "Get the app" to workers whose app already sends their spot. HELP showed no steps at all.

## What changed

  - 2+ steps: numbered `1.`/`2.`. 1 step: no number. 0 steps: the first line is "You're all set." instead of "You're in.", and no step lines.

- HELP now shows the same open steps as START.
