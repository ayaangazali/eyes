## Why
Eyes sends real people to real places, so every task must be about a public, commercial or civic place before it is dispatched (§12). `createAssessTask(env)` is the gate the engine calls before any escrow; a blocked verdict becomes `unsafe_task` and its `reason` and `fix` go straight to the agent, so they are neutral and say how to rephrase.

## What
- `rules.ts`: high-precision, category-tagged regexes over `question` and `place_label` (NFKC, `\p{Default_Ignorable_Code_Point}` stripped, curly apostrophes normalized, whitespace collapsed), in severity order: illegal_or_dangerous, following, private_property, vehicle, private_residence, identifiable_people, private_person. First match wins; per-category `reason`/`fix` templates.
- `index.ts`: rules first (a block is final, `layer: "rules"`); with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `claude-haiku-5-5` via `messages.parse` + `zodOutputFormat`, effort `low`, 5 s request timeout, 1 retry, 12 s overall `AbortSignal.timeout` so a stalled body also fails closed. No key: rules only, one warning at creation. Any classifier failure fails closed (`other`, "temporarily unavailable", "Nothing was charged. Retry..."). A model refusal blocks as `other`.
- `evals/safety/fixtures.json`: 143 cases (47 allow, 96 block, every category; 133 carry `rules_expected`), including every verifier false-positive repro and negated photo instruction as a rules allow, the §13 demo refusals (with located/number/plural variants), 8 face/person photo paraphrases and the "don't forget to photograph her face" class as rules blocks. `evals/safety/run.ts` prints precision, recall and misses; rules-only without a key; any "temporarily unavailable" verdict marks the run INVALID and is excluded. Always exits 0.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- Orchestrator ruling (fix round 1): a rules block is final, so the rule layer is high precision only. Broad patterns were deleted rather than patched with exceptions (bare `name's home`, bare bomb/drug/smuggle/surveil/trespass words, "track down", bare driveway, "house ... #N", plate mentions without a vehicle target); nuance goes to the classifier. Person objects for follow/track/watch exclude staff and "them" ("watch the barista make my latte" is allowed). Fix round 2 also dropped bare "grenade" (Grenade protein bars; it now only appears after plant/leave/build/etc.), "sell ... stolen" and bare "sneak in".
- Untrusted input: the task is `JSON.stringify`d with every `<` escaped to `\u003c` inside `<task>` tags, so a question cannot close the tag; the prompt says the block is data and an override attempt is itself a block. Only question/task_type/place_label are sent.
- Negated photo and identify instructions ("do not photograph faces", "be careful not to photograph faces", "no need to get the cashier in the shot") are skipped by a lookbehind: not/never/n't/avoid/without/no followed only by up to three of take/get/snap/a/an/any/to/need before the verb (round 4 allowlist; the verifier's list plus `to` and `need`, without which "careful not to photograph faces" and "no need to get the cashier in the shot" blocked). Any other word in between breaks the negation, so "don't forget to photograph her face" blocks.
- Photo targets are person words only and must be followed by a sentence end or a preposition/verb, so "picture of the employee schedule" is allowed. Added: `capture` verb, `the people`, and "she's / her / the cashier ... in the shot/photo/picture/frame".
- Classifier reason/fix pass through; empty values fall back to the category template.
- Tests stub global `fetch` instead of changing the `createAssessTask(env)` signature, so the real SDK request, retry, timeout and parse code runs.

## Assumptions
- `messages.parse` + `zodOutputFormat` returns `parsed_output` and throws `AnthropicError` on bad JSON or schema mismatch (`helpers/zod.js`, `lib/parser.js`); the SDK reads `globalThis.fetch` at construction.
- `claude-haiku-5-5` supports structured outputs at effort `low` with default adaptive thinking (counted in `max_tokens` 1024); refusals are HTTP 200 `stop_reason: "refusal"` with no server-side fallback.

## Known gaps
- Classifier precision/recall unmeasured until `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` exists; run `pnpm safety:eval`.
- Leetspeak, plurals, paraphrases, homoglyphs and non-English text get past the rules and rely on the classifier; rules-only mode (no key) is weaker by design and allows anything the rules miss; the composition root will refuse live mode without a key (T2.4).
- Classifier-only by design: double negatives ("Do not leave without photographing the cashier", "Don't come back without a photo of her face") look like "take a picture of the line without photographing faces" to a regex, and descriptive requests ("send a picture where she's clearly visible") have no precise surface form.
- Accepted rare false positives: "Apartment 3 leasing office" (apartment-number rule) and "Who Lives in a Pineapple" shirts (`who lives` rule).
