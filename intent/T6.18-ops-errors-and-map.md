## Why
The operator saw the /ops map as "blocked by OpenStreetMap" and an errors panel full of noise.

## What
- Leaflet `tileLayer` sends `referrerPolicy: "strict-origin-when-cross-origin"`. The pages stay `Referrer-Policy: same-origin`; OSM refuses tile requests with no Referer.
- HTTP log: 404 logs as info (scanners). Requests with User-Agent `eyes-smoke/…` (smoke:live's deliberate unsigned webhooks and bad OAuth) log as info with "(smoke probe)". Other 4xx stay warn, 5xx stay error.
- Nominatim: identifying User-Agent and a 1 request/s throttle across all lookups in the process.
- `node --no-experimental-webstorage` in the Dockerfile and mcp start/dev removes Node's localStorage warning.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- Kept OSM tiles (fixed Referer) rather than switching to CARTO: smallest change, same attribution.
- Known ceiling: the throttle is per process. A shared limiter is needed if the container scales out.

## Verification
- `apps/mcp/src/server.test.ts`: 404 → info, unauthenticated 401 → warn, the same 401 with the smoke UA → info "(smoke probe)".
- `apps/mcp/src/geo.test.ts`: User-Agent header, lookups spaced 1.1 s apart.
