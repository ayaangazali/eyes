## Why
Manufact's publishing audit of the keyless sandbox (audit 6b697ea4, end-to-end plus policy review, 2026-10-17 ~15:55 PT) flagged: "dispute_answer is labeled destructiveHint: false, but its description says it holds a worker payout when possible and moves the task to human review. This is a state-changing, potentially difficult-to-reverse workflow". The same review had made the same point about `ask_eyes`, fixed in #91.

## What
- `dispute_answer` annotations: `destructiveHint: true`. `readOnlyHint`, `idempotentHint` and `openWorldHint` are unchanged.
- The tools/list test asserts it.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- `true`, not `false`: a dispute can hold a worker's payout and only an operator resolves it, so it is not an additive write. Host apps such as ChatGPT may now ask the user before an agent disputes, which is the right default for taking money away from a worker.

## Assumptions
- No client behaviour depends on the old value besides consent prompts.
