## Why
Eyes had no front door: agents and workers landed on a tunnel host and an InsForge Sites URL. One domain with three names gives judges a landing page, workers a short app link, and agents a stable API base that survives moving the server off a laptop.
The connection entry must work in ChatGPT and Claude on the web: a raw MCP URL, current custom-connector steps, and OAuth handled on Eyes, not a curl command.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- Zone: the user's purchased `eyes.pictures`, on the same Cloudflare account. `saintess.tech` was a temporary stand-in (the one zone whose apex, `app` and `api` names were unused); it no longer serves the landing (no A record at 1.1.1.1 on 2026-10-17).
- A proxy Worker instead of a custom domain on the tunnel: Inkbox tunnel hosts are fixed per identity, and the proxy keeps `api.` stable when `ORIGIN` moves.
- Design followed the taste skill v2 read: "landing for agent builders and hackathon judges, calm street-documentary language, Eyes UI tokens, real photography, restrained motion" (variance 7, motion 4, density 3). No em dashes, no three equal cards, one accent.
- The connect command pins `--protocol legacy`. Manufact's docs show `--auth <token>`, but mcp-use 2.10.1 has no such flag (bearer goes in `-H "Authorization: Bearer ..."`), and its default `auto` protocol sends `server/discover` then a long-lived `subscriptions/listen` request, and `client connect` times out after 10 s waiting on it (seen directly and through a logging proxy; why the listen never completes is a server-side question, not changed here). The legacy `initialize` handshake connects in about 2 s. The version is pinned (`npx -y mcp-use@2.10.1`) because that is the one verified.
- `build.format: "file"` so the pages build to `terms.html` and `privacy.html`, which Workers static assets serve at `/terms` and `/privacy` without a trailing-slash redirect.
- Button labels: `.ui-button` becomes `inline-block` with `text-box: trim-both cap alphabetic` and padding back to its min-height, landing only (`packages/ui` untouched). `text-box-trim` does not apply to the `inline-flex` box `@eyes/ui` uses, so the trim alone had no effect. Browsers without `text-box` keep the `@eyes/ui` layout.
