## Why
Issue #33: a worker gets one "paid" text per task and nothing else. A worker chasing a missing payout had nowhere to see what was paid, what is still owed, or that a payout is stuck waiting for them to set up payouts.

## What
- `e:<worker_id>` link token (7-day expiry) and `EyesService.workerEarnings(token)`: paid and pending totals plus the 50 most recently claimed tasks with place, status, bounty, payout state (`paid`, `pending`, `waiting_for_payout_setup` or null), claimed and paid dates. Cents from the ledger, USD in the output.
- `012_worker_earnings.sql`: one read function, `eyes_worker_earnings(uuid)`, scoped to the worker, EXECUTE revoked from public, anon and authenticated.
- `GET /w/earnings/:token` (`HTTP_ROUTES.worker_earnings`): server-rendered page in packages/payments with the owner console's shell, escaping and private-link headers (no-store, no-referrer, CSP). Invalid, expired or wrong-kind links get a 404 page that says to text HELP.
- The paid text and the HELP reply end with `Your earnings: <url>`. `Messaging.sendPaid` takes `earnings_url`; `paidText` in packages/comms appends it.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
See ADR-020. In short: 7-day expiry because the page is a location history and a fresh link is one text away; server-rendered next to the owner console rather than a worker-web page fetching JSON; read-only with no payout setup link, since a connect link attaches whoever opens it as the payee.
- The task card lists packages/engine for "the two text templates", but the paid text template is `paidText` in packages/comms behind core's `Messaging.sendPaid`. Composing it in the engine through `sendText` would drop the Inkbox idempotency key `paid:<task>:<phone>`, so the URL is passed to `sendPaid` instead (providers.ts, inkbox.ts and its test).
- No new error code: `offer_invalid` with a message and fix for this link, as `workerConnectUrl` already does.
- `CONNECT_LINK_MS` renamed `WORKER_LINK_MS`; both worker links share the 7 days.
- The `pending` label also tells the worker to finish payout setup, because the Stripe account id is stored when the connect link is opened, before onboarding finishes (verifier finding, fix round 1).

## Assumptions
- A text sent after the worker has replied may carry links and is limited to 1600 characters; only cold iMessages are limited to 159 characters with no links (docs/vendor/inkbox.md, Limits). The paid text and the HELP reply are always replies (the worker texted START, then YES or HELP).

## Verification
- `packages/engine/src/engine.int.test.ts` (Postgres, fakes), 3 new tests and 1 changed:
  - One worker: a verified task without payout setup shows pending 3 and `waiting_for_payout_setup`. After onboarding pays it, a second task whose transfer fails shows paid 3, pending 4.25, the rows `pending` and `paid` with `paid_at`, and the earnings link is the one from the paid text's `earnings_url`. Output parses as `WorkerEarnings`.
  - Three workers on two tasks: each sees only their own task and pending total; a worker who was offered both and claimed none sees nothing.
  - Token confusion: `t:`, `o:`, `c:`, `p:` tokens with the worker id, another secret, a `.x` suffix, an unknown worker and garbage are all refused with the earnings message. A HELP link opens until 7 days minus 1 s and is refused 1 s after 7 days.
  - HELP: the text is the old reply plus ` Your earnings: <url>`, the URL opens the worker's (empty) earnings, at most 1600 characters.
- `packages/comms/src/inkbox.test.ts`: the longest paid text ($49.99, a 40-character place, a real `e:` token on a 46-character host) ends with the link and is at most 1600 characters.
- `packages/payments/src/index.test.ts`: the page shows both totals, each row's payout label (including the pending row's setup hint) and paid time, escapes an `<img onerror>` place label, sends the private-link headers, and answers a bad link with a 404 page carrying the HELP fix.
- Old code fails the new tests: with the engine, comms and payments sources from origin/main and no 012, all 7 new or changed tests fail (engine 4/35 on a fresh database, unit 3/161).
- Mutants killed on the SQL function: pending without the worker filter fails 3 engine tests; the task list without the worker filter fails 4.
- `pnpm verify:fast` pass (unit 500, contract 11, safety 139, intent ok). `DATABASE_URL=.../eyes_t417 pnpm test:integration` 89/89 on a fresh database. live-verified: no.
- The page was rendered from the route with fake data and screenshotted at 390 and 1280 px (linked in the PR); before this change the same URL answered `404 Not Found`.

## Known gaps
- A worker who texted STOP gets no HELP reply, so they must text START before they can get a fresh earnings link.
- Times on the page are UTC, like the owner console.
- The page is not linked from the "your payout is waiting" text or the capture page.
