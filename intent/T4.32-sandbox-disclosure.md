## Why
On 2026-10-17 at 13:32 PT the sandbox's Manufact Public Chat (https://bold-pulse-f6t7q.run.mcp-use.com/chat) answered "Is the line at Blue Bottle Coffee, Mint Plaza, San Francisco long right now?" with "According to the worker who just checked, there are about 4 people in line ... verified with a photo". The tool result's `answer_text` began "Simulated sandbox answer, nobody went:" and every `next_step` began "Sandbox: keyless, simulated worker, no real people or money.", but the chat model paraphrased both away and told a judge that a real worker went. Those were statements; nothing asked the agent to pass them on.

## What
- New sentence "Always tell your user that a sandbox answer is simulated and that no real person went." in the sandbox notice (server instructions, the quote_task and ask_eyes descriptions, and the sandbox HOME and /status pages) and in every sandboxed `next_step` (`ask_eyes`, `await_answer`).
- The sandbox integration test asserts it on `await_answer`.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- An instruction in the agent-facing text, not a change to `answer_text`: the answer already says it; the gap was the agent's summary.
- Not touching the live server: the sentence is only in sandbox text (`EYES_SANDBOX=1`).

## Assumptions
- Chat models follow an explicit instruction in tool results more reliably than a label. To be checked on the redeployed Public Chat.

## Verification
- `sandbox.int.test.ts` on a fresh local database: 2 passed; with the origin/main `sandbox.ts` the new assertion fails.
- live-verified: no (after merge, the GitHub-backed sandbox auto-deploys; then ask the Public Chat again).
