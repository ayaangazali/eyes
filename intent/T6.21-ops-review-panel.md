## Why
The operator could only settle a needs_review or disputed task with `pnpm resolve-task` on a laptop.

## What
- /ops: a needs_review or disputed row (Ops queue and Recent tasks) is a button. It opens a panel with the question, place, worker distance and radius, photo, answer, the three checks ("Photo is safe to share" third), the moderation reason, the agent's dispute reason, bounty and fee in escrow, an optional note and two buttons: Release (pay the worker) and Refund (return to the agent). Each needs a second click within 5 s.
- `GET /ops/tasks/:id` (operator session): the panel's data from `eyes_task_detail`.
- `POST /ops/tasks/:id/resolve` (operator session + same Origin) `{ outcome: "release" | "refund", note? }` calls `resolveTask`, the same path as `pnpm resolve-task`. It returns `{ task_id, status, already_resolved }`; a second click on a settled task returns `already_resolved: true` and moves no money. The event log records operator, action and task id.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- No new SQL: `eyes_resolve_task` already refuses a settled task with its status, which the route reads as "already resolved".
- The row leaves the queue on the next 10 s poll, not instantly: one poll loop, no second refresh path.

## Verification
- `apps/mcp/src/ops-auth.test.ts` "ops review panel": 401 without a session, 403 from another Origin, 400 on a bad outcome, release once then already_resolved, other failures pass through.
- `apps/mcp/src/ops-review.int.test.ts` (local Postgres): a held task shows in the detail route; release pays once; a second click (refund) moves nothing and the agent balance is unchanged.
