## Why
Agents spend from a prepaid balance a human funds through Stripe Checkout; workers are paid by a Transfer to their Connect account. This package is the only code that talks to Stripe: it turns verified webhooks into ledger credits and onboarding events and serves the links humans click (`/topup/:token`, `/w/connect/:token`), so the judge sees funds in and out while escrow and refunds stay in our ledger.

## What
- `createStripePayments(env)`: card-only Checkout top-up labelled `metadata.agent_id`, `metadata.topup_cents`, PaymentIntent `transfer_group` `topup_<agent_id>`; worker Connect account; Account Link (`currently_due`); Transfer with `transfer_group` `task_<task_id>`, `metadata.task_id` and the caller's idempotency key; `parseWebhook` verified over the raw body.
- `stripeRoutes(deps, env)`: `POST /webhooks/stripe`, `GET /topup/done|cancel`, `GET /topup/:token` (5..100 USD clamp), `GET /w/connect/done`, `GET /w/connect/:token`; explicit paths, done pages before `:token`.
- `scripts/fund-platform.ts`: `pm_card_bypassPending` PaymentIntent so test transfers have available balance (ADR-004); refuses when the API reports `livemode`.

## Decisions & alternatives rejected
- Accounts v1 with `controller` (application pays fees and bears losses, Express dashboard), US individual, `transfers` capability, idempotency key `eyes-worker-account-<worker_id>`: the recommendation in docs/vendor/stripe.md "Contradictions with our plan" item 1 and "### 3. Connected account for workers". `type: "express"` is deprecated in the stripe 23.0.0 `.d.ts`; Accounts v2 can be blocked per merchant. Fallback if the sandbox rejects v1: v2 `configuration.recipient` plus v2 account links (docs/vendor/stripe.md "### 4. Account Link").
- Optional `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET`: an endpoint gets platform or connected-account events, never both (`WebhookEndpointCreateParams.connect`), each with its own secret; workers' `account.updated` needs the Connect one.
- Prefill only `individual.phone`: the JOIN name may be a nickname, not a legal name. Redirect errors render escaped HTML with the EyesError status; humans open these links.
- Verifier-driven (round 1): card-only Checkout instead of handling async payments, via `allowed_payment_method_types` because stripe 23.0.0 removed `payment_method_types` from Checkout (CHANGELOG 23.0.0); vendor-note labels; `worker.onboarded` only on `transfers === "active"` from a connected account; top-ups only from platform USD sessions; no webhook secret answers a generic 500 and logs the cause.

## Assumptions
- Param names come from the stripe 23.0.0 `.d.ts`, not a live call (docs/vendor/stripe.md "Open questions"). A US individual can hold only `transfers`.
