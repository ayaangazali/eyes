- [minor] apps/mcp/src/server.ts:362: the throttle key is the raw right-most X-Forwarded-For entry, with no length cap and no IPv6 prefix grouping. Any client that reaches the app without the gateway picks its own key, and that includes an empty `X-Forwarded-For:`, which counts as present (http.ts:94) and keys on "". Key memory is bounded at 10,000 entries but grows with key length: +80.2 MB with 8 KB entries at both 10k and 20k keys, against +3.6 MB with IP-sized keys. Node's 16 KB header cap allows about 160 MB. IPv6 clients can also rotate through their /64. The intent's Known gaps covers the direct-reach and IPv6 parts but not the memory cost.
