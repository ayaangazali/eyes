FINDINGS:
- [blocker] packages/engine/src/index.ts:290-291 (with intent/T4.9-payouts.md "Release on every outcome" and the ADR-012 addendum Consequences): payOut always releases the lease in `finally`, including when the outcome of the transfer POST is unknown (network failure on all tries, 5xx, 409 idempotency_error). The next trigger (30 s retry tick, account.updated webhook, capture or resolve) then starts a new attempt with a fresh key while Stripe may still execute or reconcile the first POST. The transfer_group lookup finds nothing, so a second transfer is created. origin/main reused `payout:<task_id>`, so Stripe replayed the first result for the same sequence. This is a double-pay regression. It is listed under Known gaps, but money findings cannot be deferred under ADR-010. The intent's reason for not holding the lease is that holding "would delay the onboarding webhook up to 15 minutes after a capability failure". That mixes up two cases: a capability failure is thrown before the lookup and its outcome is known, while an unknown POST outcome is a different case and can be handled separately. Repro (scratchpad v57-attack.int.test.ts, S2b): the fake Stripe runs the POST 4 s after the client's 3 tries have all failed with a network error. Capture on an active account leaves the task `verified` with release.stripe_ref null. An immediate B.markWorkerOnboarded(acct) marks it paid. 4.5 s later the fake holds 2 transfers in `task_<id>` and the ledger has 1 payout row. The same harness on origin/main code: 1 transfer. Suggested fix: have payoutWorker tag errors from transfers.create whose outcome is unknown (StripeConnectionError, StripeAPIError, idempotency_error), and have payOut skip eyes_payout_end for them, so the next attempt and its lookup wait out the 15-minute TTL. Capability errors and 4xx refusals keep releasing at once, so the onboarding webhook is not delayed.
- [minor] packages/payments/src/index.ts:34 and packages/db/migrations/009_payout_retry.sql:14 (money, so blocking under ADR-010): the 15-minute TTL protects against double pay, not only against a stuck task. An attempt that stalls for more than 15 minutes between its lookup and its POST is overtaken by the next attempt. The ADR's 12.2-minute worst case depends on stripe-node defaults (80 s timeout, 2 network retries) that `new Stripe(key)` does not set. Repro (S6): hold the first POST, age the lease by 15m01s in SQL, then run B.markWorkerOnboarded. B gets the lease, the lookup is empty and it creates T2. Releasing the hold creates T1: 2 transfers and 1 payout row (origin/main: 1 transfer). Suggested fix: pass `{ timeout: 80_000, maxNetworkRetries: 2 }` to `new Stripe` explicitly, so a library upgrade cannot push an attempt past the TTL.
- [minor] packages/engine/src/index.ts:859-866 and packages/db/migrations/009_payout_retry.sql:27-36 (not money, deferrable): the starvation is fixed, but the second half of #52 is not: inactive accounts are still polled on every pass of the cursor. With 25 or fewer stuck tasks that is every 30 s tick, forever. Each visit costs 1 accounts.retrieve and 2 UPDATEs of the release ledger row (begin and end). Repro (S4): 30 never-onboarded tasks plus 1 payable task. The payable task is paid on tick 1, and the stuck accounts get 24, 6, 25 and 5 GETs on ticks 1-4. Suggested fix (follow-up): add a per-instance backoff for accounts found inactive, on top of the cursor. markWorkerOnboarded bypasses it, and the cursor already prevents the starvation the ADR uses to reject a skip list.
