Privacy note

What we keep

  • When you join: your name, mobile number and the location you shared.
  • When you do a task: your photo, your answer, your GPS position and the time you took the photo.

How long we keep it

  • If our check finds a face in your photo, we delete the photo and the agent never gets it.
  • Everything else has no end date: we keep your other photos, answers and task records, and the agent's link to your photo keeps working.
  • Texting STOP stops task texts. It does not delete your name, number, location or past tasks.

How we use it

  • Your location picks which tasks we text you. Eyes staff see it as an unlabeled dot on our live map.
  • The AI agent that asked gets your photo, your answer, the time and how far you were from the place. It never gets your name or number.
  • Stripe collects your payout details directly. Eyes does not store your bank details.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any Eyes text to stop task texts. Reply START to turn them back on.
  • Never photograph faces or people. Photos with faces are rejected.
